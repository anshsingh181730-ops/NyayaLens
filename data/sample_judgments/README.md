# Sample Judgments Directory

This directory is designated for authentic judicial orders, rulings, and judgments from the Supreme Court of India and High Courts.

## Available Sample Judgments in Repository
You can find pre-placed authentic landmark Supreme Court judgments in `/sample_judgements/` and `/data/sample_judgments/`:
- `Arnesh_Kumar_2014.PDF` - Landmark judgment on arrest guidelines under Section 498A IPC & Section 41 CrPC.
- `D_K_Basu_1994.PDF` - Landmark judgment on custodial violence, detention, and arrestee rights.
- `Kesavananda-Bharati-1973.pdf` - Historic constitutional bench judgment on the Basic Structure Doctrine.
- `Lalita_Kumari_2013.PDF` - Constitution Bench judgment on mandatory registration of FIR under Section 154 CrPC.
- `Maneka Gandhi.pdf` - Constitutional landmark on Article 21 and substantive due process.

## Supported Input Files
Users may upload any PDF file or paste text into the NyayaLens Workspace or Citation Audit tool:
- Petitions (Special Leave Petitions, Writ Petitions)
- Orders & Judgments (Trial Court, High Court, Supreme Court)
- Written Submissions, Counter Affidavits, Draft Filings
